The report could have used data from the table to illustrate each of the three major causes shown in the pie graph as they were reported, removing the need to provide the reasoning given in the conclusion and earlier paragraphs. Most visualks allow scope for explanation of what is being represented so it is unhelpful to slavishly rely on the provided labels and assume that the reader will understand what is meant by them.

When there are few statistics to report more words can be allocated to descriptions of key diagrammatic elements.
